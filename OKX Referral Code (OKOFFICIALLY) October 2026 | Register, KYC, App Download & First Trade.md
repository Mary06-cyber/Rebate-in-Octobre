## 5. Why Does OKX KYC Fail? How to Improve Approval Rate?

The most common KYC failure reasons involve documents, name matching, photos, and facial recognition.

### 1. Name Doesn't Match Documents
The name entered should match your ID exactly. Don't use nicknames, alternate spellings, or someone else's name.

### 2. Expired Documents
Use documents within their valid period.

### 3. Blurry or Glare-affected Photos
When photographing, ensure: complete edges, clear text, no severe glare, no hand obstructions, no editing or beautification.

### 4. Facial Recognition Failure
Perform facial recognition in a well-lit area and avoid:
- Wearing sunglasses;
- Hats obscuring the face;
- Blurry camera lens;
- Overly dark environment;
- Using someone else's face.
