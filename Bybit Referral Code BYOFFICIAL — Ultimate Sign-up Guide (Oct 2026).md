## Chapter 10: Derivatives

### 10.1 Three flavors

| Type | Pricing | Settle | Max leverage |
|------|---------|--------|--------------|
| USDT perp | USDT | USDT | 100× |
| USDC perp | USDC | USDC | 50× |
| Inverse / coin-margined | USD | BTC | 75× |

### 10.2 Leverage math
- 10× → ±10% move = ±100% P&L
- 50× → ±2% move = liquidation
- 100× → ±1% move = liquidation

> 🧨 Newbies: stay at ≤ 3×.

### 10.3 Liquidation price
```
Liq = Entry × (1 − 1/leverage × MMR)
```
Example: entry 60,000, 10×, MMR 0.5% → liq ≈ 57,000. A 5% drop liquidates you.

### 10.4 Cross vs Isolated margin

| Mode | Cross | Isolated |
|------|-------|----------|
| Risk pool | Whole account | Single position |
| Best for | Whales / hedgers | Beginners |

Tip: beginners = Isolated + low leverage.
