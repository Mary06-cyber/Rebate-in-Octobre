## 3. KYC Identity Verification

An unverified account can log in and view markets, but **cannot withdraw, has limited fiat access, and has restricted derivatives limits**. KYC is mandatory.

### 3.1 Where to find it

- App: Assets → settings icon (top right) → Identity Verification
- Web: Avatar → Account & Security → Identity Verification

### 3.2 Two levels

| Level | Documents required | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Country/region, legal name, ID type (ID / passport), ID number, expiry date, liveness face scan | Withdrawals, deposits, fiat purchase, derivatives trading |
| Advanced | Proof of address dated within 3 months (utility bill / bank statement / residence certificate) | Higher withdrawal limits, extra fiat channels and campaigns |

**For spot and futures trading, Standard is enough.**

### 3.3 Five reasons KYC gets rejected

1. **Glare or blur on the document photo** — lay it flat on a dark surface, shoot in natural light, keep all four corners visible.
2. **Name order reversed** — match your document exactly; don't reorder to Western convention.
3. **Mismatched details** — one wrong character in name, ID number or date of birth means rejection.
4. **Face scan failure** — remove glasses and hats, face the light source, avoid backlight or darkness.
5. **Document near expiry** — keep at least 3 months of validity.

Review usually takes a few minutes to one business day. If rejected, resubmit using the on-screen reason; you don't need a new account.

> **Important:** the name on your KYC document must match the name on the bank account you later pay from. A mismatch can get orders refused.
