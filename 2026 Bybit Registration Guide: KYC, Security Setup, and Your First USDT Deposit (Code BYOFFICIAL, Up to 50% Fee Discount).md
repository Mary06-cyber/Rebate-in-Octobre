## 4. Identity verification (KYC)

Bybit enforces **mandatory verification**: at least standard-level identity verification is required to use all products and to withdraw.

### Standard verification (required)

1. Go to Identity Verification
2. Select the issuing country/region and ID type (national ID / passport / driver's licence)
3. Upload photos of both sides of the document
4. Complete the liveness face check
5. Wait for review — usually minutes to one business day

### Advanced verification (optional)

Adds proof of address (utility bill, bank statement or residence certificate from the last 3 months) to raise withdrawal limits. Standard is enough when you're starting out.

### Five common rejection reasons

1. Glare, blur or shadows on the photo
2. Document corners cut off
3. Expired document
4. Name on the document doesn't match the account
5. Face check in a dark room, or with a hat / glasses covering the face

## 6. Your first USDT deposit, end to end

This is the core of this guide. Pick one of three routes.

### Route 1: C2C buy (most common for beginners — buy directly with fiat)

1. On web or app, go to **Buy Crypto** → **C2C**
2. Select **USDT**, enter the amount (or the fiat amount you want to spend)
3. Pick a merchant by **completion rate, number of completed orders and online status** — favour high-reputation merchants
4. Click Buy and check the supported payment methods and the payment time limit
5. Transfer using the details shown on the page — **keep all communication inside the platform; anyone asking to move to WhatsApp / Telegram is scamming you**
6. After transferring, click "Paid" and **keep a screenshot of the payment proof**
7. Wait for the merchant to release the coins (usually minutes). **Until the platform shows "Completed", don't trust any verbal claim that coins were released**
8. Funds arrive in your **Funding Account**

⚠️ **Six C2C safety rules**: only high-completion, well-rated merchants; keep chat inside the platform; never pay outside the platform; click "Paid" and keep proof; never release coins before payment is confirmed; split large amounts into several orders.

### Route 2: On-chain deposit (moving USDT from another platform or wallet)

1. Go to **Assets** → **Deposit** → **Deposit Crypto**
2. Select the coin **USDT**
3. **Choose the network** — this is where most mistakes happen; see the rules below
4. Copy the deposit address (or scan the QR code)
5. Initiate the withdrawal from your other platform/wallet: paste the address, choose the **same network**, enter the amount
6. Wait for confirmations. Funds also land in your Funding Account

⚠️ **Three hard rules for on-chain deposits:**
- **The network must match**: if the address is TRC20, the sending side must also be TRC20. Wrong network = lost funds, usually unrecoverable
- **Copy the full address**: test with a small amount (e.g. 20 USDT) first, then send the rest once it arrives
- **Mind the minimum deposit and network congestion**: below the minimum, nothing arrives

**Which network to pick**: TRC20 (Tron) is cheap and fast — the default choice for smaller amounts. ERC20 (Ethereum) costs more but is the most widely supported. In the end, use whatever your sending platform supports.

### Route 3: Transfer from another exchange

Same as Route 2, except the sender is another exchange. Use the same network and paste your Bybit deposit address.

### Final step: move funds to the Unified Trading Account (this is where people get stuck)

Bybit uses a **Unified Trading Account (UTA)**. **Funds that arrive sit in your Funding Account and cannot be used to place orders yet** — you have to transfer them:

1. Go to **Assets** → **Transfer**
2. From: **Funding Account**
3. To: **Unified Trading Account (UTA)**
4. Select USDT and enter the amount (or all)
5. Confirm — the transfer is instant

Only after this will your Spot or Futures page show an available balance you can trade with.
