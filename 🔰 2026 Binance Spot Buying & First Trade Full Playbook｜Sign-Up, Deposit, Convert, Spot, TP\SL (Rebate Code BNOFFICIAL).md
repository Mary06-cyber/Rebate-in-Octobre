## 🟢 5. Common Spot Order Types

| Type | Execution logic | Best for | Downside |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Market | Fills immediately at best available price | Quick entry, small trial | Slippage in volatility |
| Limit | Fills at your price or better | Defined entry/exit, scaling | May never fill if price misses |
| Stop-Limit | When stop triggers, places a limit order | Planned stop-loss, breakout follow | Fast moves may partially fill |
| Stop-Market | When stop triggers, handles as market | Prioritize exiting fast | Slippage larger than limit |
| OCO | Take-profit limit + stop-limit together; one fills, other cancels | Manage both exits automatically | More setup complexity |
| Trailing Stop | Trails favorable price by set distance, triggers on reversal | Lock trend profits | May trigger early in choppy markets |

Beginners should master Market and Limit first, then Stop-Limit; use OCO and Trailing after reading the order book comfortably.
