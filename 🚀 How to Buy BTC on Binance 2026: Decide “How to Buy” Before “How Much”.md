## 一、Confirm you want BTC Spot

If your goal is to own Bitcoin and see **BTC Balance** in your account, the normal choice is **BTC Spot**.

Spot = you buy the asset directly.  
Futures = you open a contract based on BTC price, not actual BTC ownership.

## 二、Spot vs Futures

| Item | Spot | Futures |
|---|---|---|
| Own BTC? | Yes | No |
| Leverage | No | Yes |
| Liquidation risk | No | Yes |
| Good for beginner | Yes | Later |

For a first BTC purchase, Spot is simpler and safer to learn.

## 三、You don’t need 1 full BTC

BTC is divisible. You can buy with:

- 20 USDT
- 50 USDT
- 100 USDT
- 500 USDT
- any amount you choose

You only need the budget you set.

## 四、Decide budget, not BTC quantity

Beginners should decide:

> How much USDT do I want to spend?

Example: BTC budget = 300 USDT.  
After fill, the system converts it into BTC based on actual price.

## 五、Why budget first matters

Without budget, people often do:

price up → fear missing out → add funds  
price down → want to average down → overspend

Set a **maximum BTC budget** before trading.

## 六、Common pair: BTCUSDT

If you hold USDT, check **BTCUSDT**.

- BTC = asset you buy
- USDT = asset you pay

After buy: USDT decreases, BTC increases.

## 七、Check if USDT is usable in Spot

Sometimes USDT exists in:

- Funding
- Futures
- Earn
- other wallets

If Spot order says insufficient balance, transfer USDT to Spot first.

## 八、Internal transfer if funds already on Binance

Example: USDT in Funding → Spot.

This is an internal **Transfer**, not an on-chain withdrawal.

## 九、Deposit if funds are external

If USDT is in another exchange or private wallet, use **Deposit**.

Check:

- Asset: USDT
- Deposit address
- Network

## 十、Network must match

Do not assume “both are USDT” is enough.  
Sending network and receiving network must match, or funds may not arrive.

## 十一、Open BTCUSDT after funding

Trade → Spot → search BTCUSDT.

Check:

- Current price

- Volume
- Order book
- Recent trades

## 十二、Two order types are enough at first

- **Market Order**
- **Limit Order**

## 十三、Market Order = fast execution

Choose Market if you want immediate fill.

Example: spend 200 USDT.

Buy BTC → Market → enter 200 USDT → confirm.

## 十四、Market may fill at multiple prices

A market order matches available sell orders.  
Check **Average Fill Price** after execution.

## 十五、When slippage is bigger

- fast price movement
- thin order book
- large order size

Actual average price may differ from the price shown before click.

## 十六、Limit Order = set your price

Use Limit if you only want to buy at a certain price.

Example: market is high, set lower limit price and wait.

## 十七、Limit may not fill

If price never reaches your limit, order stays **Open**.  
That is not an error.

## 十八、Open ≠ filled

Status types:

- Submitted
- Open
- Partially Filled
- Filled

Only **Filled** means purchase complete.

## 十九、One order or分批

Example budget 1000 USDT:

- one order 1000
- or 250 × 4

Both are fine.

## 二十、分批 ≠ guaranteed cheaper

DCA reduces timing risk, but if price keeps rising, later buys are more expensive.

## 二十一、Follow budget, not emotion

If plan is 1200 USDT in 4 parts, don’t suddenly change to 3000 USDT because of FOMO.

## 二十二、FOMO is the common trap

Seeing BTC pump → “I must buy now” → oversize position.

That is FOMO.

## 二十三、Pump doesn’t mean safer

Quick rally may also mean:

- high volatility
- chasing price
- fast pullback

## 二十四、Dump doesn’t mean must buy more

BTC can drop because of macro risk, liquidations, liquidity, news.  
Down ≠ automatic buy.

## 二十五、Where to see BTC after buy

Assets → Spot:

- BTC Balance
- Available BTC
- Estimated Value

## 二十六、Balance vs Available

If part of BTC is in a sell order or locked product, Available BTC is lower.

## 二十七、Not mandatory to withdraw immediately

If you just hold or trade inside Binance, Spot is fine.

## 二十八、Withdraw only for self-custody

Withdraw when you want:
