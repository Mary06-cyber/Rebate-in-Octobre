## 二、20%, 40%, 50%, 80% — who actually receives it?
Referral systems have two sides: the **referrer** who gives the code and the **invited user** who enters it. Promotional pages often mix the numbers.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 80% wallet/referrer commission | Special referral products, not standard user trading rebate | Referrer |
| “Up to $600 new-user bundle” | Campaign vouchers, task-based, independent of referral code | You, if tasks met |

Key decoding points:
- Invited-user rebate and referrer commission are separate. A referrer may earn 40% or 50% while sharing only 10%–20% with you. [4](@ref)

- Any ad saying “you get 40% or 50% trading-fee rebate” should be treated as false until proven in your dashboard.
