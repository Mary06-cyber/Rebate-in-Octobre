## 1. Why is it worth building a Trading Journal for Futures?

Because human memory:

is prone to deviations.

Profitable trades:

are usually remembered very clearly.

Consecutive losses:

you might only remember the last one.

And many people easily mistake:

"Making money on this trade"

for:

"This set of operations must be correct."

## 2. A Trading Journal is not a diary

A truly effective trading journal:

is not about writing every day:

Today BTC was very strong.

Today the market was bad.

Today my emotions were average.

Instead:

it breaks down trades into statistical data.

## 93. FAQ

**Q: Is it necessary for Binance Futures beginners to keep a Trading Journal?**
A: Very worthwhile. A trading journal helps you distinguish whether problems come from Strategy, Entry, Position Size, costs, or Discipline, rather than relying solely on memory.

**Q: What is the minimum to record in a Trading Journal?**
A: At least record Pair, Direction, Entry, Stop, Position Size, Exit, Fee, Funding, Realized PnL, and Setup.

**Q: Is recording only PnL enough?**
A: Not enough. PnL only tells you the result, not why you made or lost money.

**Q: What is R Multiple?**
A: It's a method to compare trade results against planned risk, allowing trades of different sizes to be analyzed on the same scale.

**Q: What is MFE used for?**
A: It observes the maximum favorable fluctuation during a trade, helping analyze if exits were too early or too late.

**Q: What is MAE used for?**
A: It observes the maximum adverse fluctuation, helping analyze Entry, Stop, and normal market fluctuations.

**Q: Is a higher Win Rate always better?**
A: Not necessarily. You must also consider Average Win, Average Loss, Fees, Drawdown, and Expectancy.

**Q: How often should I review?**
A: You can divide into Daily, Weekly, Monthly layers. Check execution daily, statistics weekly, and evaluate strategy monthly.
