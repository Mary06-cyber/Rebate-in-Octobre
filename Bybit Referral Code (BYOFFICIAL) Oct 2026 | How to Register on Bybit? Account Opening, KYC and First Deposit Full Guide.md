## 8. Why Can't USDT Be Traded Immediately After Deposit? Funding Account vs UTA

Bybit on-chain deposits currently usually arrive in:

**Funding Account**

Funding Account is mainly used for:

- crypto deposits;
- withdrawals;
- some fiat and fund management functions.

While:

**Unified Trading Account (UTA)**

is mainly used for:

- Spot;
- Spot Margin;
- Perpetual;
- Futures;
- Options and other supported trading products.

So if USDT has arrived but the trading page shows insufficient available balance, first check whether the assets are still in the Funding Account.

To transfer, go to:

**Assets → Transfer → From Funding Account → To Unified Trading Account → select USDT → enter amount → Confirm**

This operation is only an internal transfer within the same Bybit account, not an on-chain withdrawal, so do not confuse it with sending USDT to an external wallet.

Bybit also lets users adjust the Deposit Route so future deposits go to Funding, UTA or other supported accounts based on settings.
