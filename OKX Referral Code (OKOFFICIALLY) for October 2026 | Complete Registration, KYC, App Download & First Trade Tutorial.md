## 9. How to Execute Your First Trade on OKX?

If your account is ready with USDT, new users can start by familiarizing themselves with the trading page through Spot trading.

For example, buying BTC with USDT:

OKX App → Trade → Spot → Search BTC/USDT → Buy BTC

Then select the order type.

### Market Order
A market order will be executed as quickly as possible at the currently available market price.

Suitable for situations where quick execution is desired, but note:

- Market orders may experience slippage
- Especially in low liquidity or highly volatile markets, the final average execution price may differ from the price seen at the moment of placing the order

### Limit Order
A limit order allows you to set the price at which you are willing to buy BTC.

For example, if BTC is currently at 80,000 USDT and you only want to buy at 78,000 USDT, you can place a limit buy order at 78,000 USDT.

The advantage is price control; the disadvantage is:

- If the market does not reach your set price, the order may remain unfilled

For first-time traders, it is recommended to use a smaller amount first to familiarize yourself with placing orders, canceling orders, order history, and asset changes.
