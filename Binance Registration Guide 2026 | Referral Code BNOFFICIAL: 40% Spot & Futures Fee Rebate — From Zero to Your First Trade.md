## 4. Identity verification (KYC)

No KYC = no deposits, no trading, no withdrawals. Do this first.

**Process:**

1. Go to "Identity Verification" on app or web
2. Select your ID issuing country/region
3. Enter name, ID number, date of birth
4. Upload front and back of your document
5. Complete the liveness face check (blink, turn head)
6. Wait — usually minutes to a few hours, up to 24h at peak

**Top rejection reasons:**

- Glare, blur, or cropped corners on the document photo
- Expired or nearly expired ID
- Face doesn't match the document photo (major hairstyle or makeup changes count)
- Network region inconsistent with ID region
- Repeated submissions in a short window

**Practical tip:** shoot in daylight against a plain wall using your phone's native camera — no beauty-filter apps. If rejected three times, wait 24 hours; resubmitting repeatedly pushes you into the manual queue, which is slower.

## 7. Spot trading basics

### Three order types

| Type | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Limit | Sits at your price until filled | Not in a hurry, want a better price |
| Market | Fills immediately at best available price | Chasing a move, need instant fill |
| Stop-limit | Places a limit order once trigger is hit | Defensive exits, controlling drawdown |

### Three rules for beginners

1. **Start with BTC / ETH only** — deepest liquidity, lowest slippage, hardest to get wicked out on.
2. **Risk no more than 10% of capital per position** — so one bad call stays survivable.
3. **Set your stop at the same time you open.** "I'll watch it" doesn't work for 24 hours.

### The detail almost every guide skips

Spot fees are deducted from the asset you receive. If you enabled "pay fees with BNB" but hold **no BNB** in your Spot wallet, the discount silently doesn't apply — you pay full price while believing you're discounted. Either keep some BNB on hand, or leave the toggle off.

## 8. Futures trading basics

### Contract types

- **USDT-margined**: USDT as collateral, PnL in USDT. Most beginner-friendly.
- **Coin-margined**: BTC/ETH as collateral, PnL in that coin.

### Concepts you must know

- **Cross vs Isolated**: isolated only risks that position's margin; cross draws on your whole balance. Beginners: isolated.
- **Margin ratio**: the lower it goes, the closer you are to liquidation.
- **Funding rate**: paid between longs and shorts, settled every 8 hours (some markets 4h or 1h). **The exchange does not keep it.**
- **Liquidation price**: hit it and the position is force-closed. No undo.

### Risk warning (read it properly)

- Leverage cuts both ways: at 10x, a 10% move against you zeroes you out
- 50x+ isn't trading, it's gambling
- Don't average down on losers, don't revenge trade, don't go all-in
- **Practice on the testnet first** until orders, stops and liquidation make sense
