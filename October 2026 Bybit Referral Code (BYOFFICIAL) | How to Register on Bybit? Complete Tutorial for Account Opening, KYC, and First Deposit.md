## 8. Why can't USDT be traded immediately after deposit? What is the difference between Funding Account and UTA?

Bybit on-chain deposits currently default to entering:

**Funding Account**

Funding Account is mainly used for:

- Cryptocurrency deposits;
- Withdrawals;
- Some fiat and fund management functions.

While:

**Unified Trading Account (UTA)**

Is mainly used for:

- Spot;
- Spot Margin;
- Perpetual;
- Futures;
- Options and other supported trading products.

So if USDT has successfully arrived, but the trading page shows insufficient available balance, first check whether the assets are still in the Funding Account.

When a transfer is needed, you can enter:

Assets → Transfer → From Funding Account → To Unified Trading Account → Select USDT → Enter amount → Confirm

This operation is only an internal asset transfer within the same Bybit account, not an on-chain withdrawal. Therefore, do not confuse it with sending USDT to an external wallet.

Bybit also supports users adjusting the Deposit Route, allowing subsequent deposits to enter Funding, UTA, or other supported accounts according to settings.
