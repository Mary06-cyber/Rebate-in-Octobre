## 3. KYC Identity Verification

An unverified account can log in and view markets, but **cannot buy via C2C, cannot withdraw, and has limited derivatives access**. KYC is mandatory.

### 3.1 Where to find it

- App: Assets → settings icon (top right) → Identity Verification
- Web: Avatar → Account & Security → Identity Verification

### 3.2 Two levels

| Level | Documents required | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard (Level 1) | Country/region, legal name, ID type (ID card / passport), ID number, expiry date, liveness face scan | C2C buying, withdrawals, deposits, derivatives trading |
| Advanced (Level 2) | Proof of address dated within 3 months (utility bill / bank statement / residence certificate) | Higher withdrawal limits, extra fiat channels and campaigns |

**For spot and futures trading, Level 1 is enough.**

### 3.3 Five reasons KYC gets rejected

1. **Glare or blur on the document photo** — lay it flat on a dark surface, shoot in natural light, keep all four corners visible.
2. **Name order reversed** — match your document exactly; don't reorder to Western convention.
3. **Mismatched details** — one wrong character in name, ID number or date of birth means rejection.
4. **Face scan failure** — remove glasses and hats, face the light source, avoid backlight or darkness.
5. **Document near expiry** — keep at least 3 months of validity.

Review usually takes a few minutes to one business day. If rejected, resubmit using the on-screen reason; you do not need a new account.

**Important:** the name on your KYC document must match the name on the bank account you later pay from in C2C. A mismatch can get orders refused or flagged.

## 5. C2C Purchase: The Most Common Way to Fund an Account

C2C (fiat) works like this: the platform holds the crypto in escrow, you pay the merchant in fiat, and the merchant releases the crypto to you. **The platform charges no fee**; the merchant sets the price.

### 5.1 Full flow

1. App home → Buy Crypto → C2C / P2P Trading
2. Switch to **Buy**, select **USDT**, enter the fiat amount
3. Filter payment method: bank transfer / e-wallet / local options
4. **Pick a merchant** — check completion rate (95%+ recommended), number of trades, account age, order limits
5. Tap Buy → open the order page showing the merchant's payment details
6. Pay **from your own account in your own name**, using the exact amount shown
7. Tap "Transferred, notify seller"
8. Wait for release (usually 1–10 minutes) → USDT lands in your Funding Account

### 5.2 Six rules that protect your money

- **Never click "Paid" before you actually paid** — it counts as confirmation and the money is gone.
- **Never trade off-platform** — if a merchant offers a better price on WhatsApp/Telegram, refuse. Escrow disappears off-platform.
- **Leave transfer notes blank** — avoid words that trigger bank risk controls.
- **Split large amounts** — one oversized transfer is more likely to be frozen; split across several orders and days.
- **Pay from an account in your own name** — third-party payment lets the merchant cancel and file a dispute.
- **Appeal if the merchant stalls** — the order page has an appeal button; the platform decides using chat logs and payment proof.

### 5.3 Don't forget the internal transfer

C2C purchases land in the Funding Account and **cannot be used for derivatives directly**.
Go to: Assets → Transfer → Funding Account → Unified Trading Account (UTA), asset USDT, amount = all.
