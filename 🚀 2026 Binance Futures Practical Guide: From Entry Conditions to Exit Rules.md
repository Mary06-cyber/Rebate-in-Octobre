## 1. Futures is not guessing direction

Correct direction + bad entry + oversized position + no stop = still lose.

## 2. Market environment first

- Trend
- Range
- Breakout
- High Volatility

## 3. Trend structure

HH/HL = bullish bias
LH/LL = bearish bias
Counter-trend trades carry higher risk.

## 4. Range market

Do not chase middle of the range. Risk/Reward is usually poor there.

## 5. Breakout needs confirmation

Check continuation, retest, fake breakdown.

## 6. Predefine trade condition

Only enter IF ______.
No condition = No Trade.

## 7. Entry Zone

Use price or zone, not emotional market orders.

## 8. Invalidation

Price level where original thesis fails.

## 9. Stop Loss from Invalidation

Invalidation → Stop → Stop Distance → Position Size

## 10. Max Loss first

Example: 2000 USDT account, max loss 20 USDT/trade.

## 11. Position Size is math

Not feeling. Based on entry-stop distance and max loss.

## 12. Leverage last

Same 10x can be very different risk depending on size.
Leverage = margin efficiency.
Position Size = real exposure.

Order:
Setup → Entry → Invalidation → SL → Max Loss → Size → Leverage

## 13. Isolated vs Cross

**Isolated**: single-position risk boundary
**Cross**: shares account balance, bigger cascade risk

## 14. Total Exposure

BTC Long + ETH Long + SOL Long may be one directional bet.
Check total exposure daily.

## 15. Market / Limit

**Market**: fast, slippage risk
**Limit**: price control
Pending orders still need management.

## 16. After open check

Pair / Side / Size / Entry / SL / TP

## 17. Mark Price

Binance liquidation uses Mark Price, not Last Price.
Liquidation line ≠ stop loss.

## 18. Take Profit

TP1/TP2/TP3 or single target.
Partial close reduces remaining risk.
Do not keep moving TP farther emotionally.

## 19. Add Position rules

Only if planned: max adds, max total size, max total loss.

## 20. Funding Rate

Perp funding often settles every 8h.
Positive funding: longs pay shorts.
High funding alone ≠ reversal.

## 21. Open Interest

Rising OI = more open contracts, not automatically "more longs".

## 22. Event Risk

CPI, FOMC, NFP, regulation news → wider spread, slippage, wicks.

## 23. Costs

Open fee + close fee + funding + spread + slippage.
Frequent scalping bleeds on cost.
